<chess-game>

A chessboard view of a PGN game. The notation is the element's text content — there is no other markup and no configuration. 

<script src="chess-game.js"></script>

<chess-game>1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 a6 1/2-1/2</chess-game>

Annotations

Comments, glyphs and variations all show in the notation. Clicking a move inside a variation follows that variation — the arrow keys then walk it rather than the mainline.

A study, from a set position

A FEN tag says where play starts, so the move numbers and the side to move come from the game rather than from move one.

[SetUp "1"] [FEN "8/6P1/8/8/8/1k6/8/K7 w - - 0 51"] 51. g8=Q+ {The pawn queens with check along the long diagonal.} Kb4 52. Qb8+ Ka4 53. Qb2 {The queen cuts the king off; the white king walks up.} 1-0

Bare movetext

No tag pairs, no result marker. Import format is lax and so is this.

1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 Bb4 4.e3 O-O 5.Bd3 d5 6.Nf3 c5

When the notation is not a game

The parser accepts any well-formed SAN move; whether it is playable is a different question, and one only a board can answer. A move that is legal notation but not a legal move stops the line and says so.

1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Qxh8 {No queen can reach h8 here.} 1-0
